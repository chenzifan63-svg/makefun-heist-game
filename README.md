Vault Breaker is an original top-down heist game: sneak through a museum at night, crack the vault, grab the loot and reach the green EXIT unseen. Guards patrol with vision cones cut by walls and crates, and fixed security cameras sweep red cones across the hall — cut a power box to kill the nearest camera, or wait in the shadows. Grabbing the loot is loud and wakes the room, so the alarm meter fills from every guard and camera that spots you. Three levels, up to five guards a floor — patrollers, fast runners with gold rings, and stationary watchers — plus coins worth a risky detour, three lives, score and ghost bonuses, and synthesized WebAudio sound with no external files.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome.
